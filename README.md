# Rare variant association study in autism
Code repository for *"Rare variation illuminates the distinct and pleiotropic genetic architecture of autism across neuropsychiatric traits"*

**DOI:** [Preprint](LINK_TO_ADD)

**Summary:** 
Autism spectrum disorder is a heritable and heterogeneous condition that shares features with other developmental and psychiatric disorders. Here, we aggregate rare protein-coding sequence variation from 62,429 individuals with autism to explore its genetic architecture and investigate overlap with other developmental, psychiatric, and population traits. We use a Bayesian framework to integrate across variant classes and inheritance modes to identify 253 autosomal genes where damaging variants are associated with autism at a false discovery rate (FDR) < 0.001, approximating exome-wide significance. As the scale of gene discovery in autism has increased, we identify genes with an increasing proportion of evidence from missense and inherited variants, reflecting more moderate penetrance. We identify subsets of genes enriched for variants in autism with or without co-occurring global developmental delay and/or intellectual disability, and we jointly analyze our cohort with 26,109 individuals independently ascertained for developmental disorders, identifying 480 genes (FDR < 0.001). We cluster the 253 autism-associated genes based on association evidence across separate large-scale studies of developmental disorders, schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, and epilepsy, and we find differences in biological process enrichments and patterns of phenotypic severity between clusters. Finally, utilizing UK Biobank and All of Us data, we characterize the effects of autism-associated genes across diverse human phenotypes and identify pleiotropic genes affecting a broad set of physiological systems. Collectively drawing upon the exomes of over 1.5 million individuals, these analyses find that rare variants across hundreds of genes contribute to autism with or without developmental comorbidities as well as complex traits in the general population.

## Description of content:

Each folder contains the script to execute the following analyses:

- **01_variant_calling:** Contains the scripts for task1 (`scrip1_name`) and task2 (`script2_name`).  

- **02_gene_discovery:** Contains the scripts for task1 (`scrip1_name`) and task2 (`script2_name`).

- **03_GO_enrichment:** Contains the scripts for task1 (`scrip1_name`) and task2 (`script2_name`).

- **04_DDID_comorbidity_analyses:** Contains the scripts for task1 (`scrip1_name`) and task2 (`script2_name`).
  
- **05_gene_clustering:** Contains the scripts for task1 (`scrip1_name`) and task2 (`script2_name`).

- **06_pleiotropy_analyses:** Contains the scripts for task1 (`scrip1_name`) and task2 (`script2_name`).
